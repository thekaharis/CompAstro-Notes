# Choudhury, Tirthankar Roy

## Role and Affiliation

Professor at NCRA–TIFR (National Centre for Radio Astrophysics, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research), Pune University Campus, Pune, India. One of India's leading theorists on the epoch of reionization and the intergalactic medium. Works at the intersection of theoretical reionization physics, observational constraints from Lyman-alpha forest measurements and 21cm observations, and semi-numerical simulation methods.

## Academic Background and Career

Choudhury received his PhD from the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research under the supervision of T. Padmanabhan, one of India's most celebrated cosmologists. His doctoral research established him as a key figure in reionization theory, with particular emphasis on understanding the physical processes that govern the ionization history of the universe and the observational signatures that can constrain this history. He has since held positions at various institutions and is now a senior faculty member at NCRA-TIFR, where he leads research on reionization physics and the ionized intergalactic medium.

The 2022 Springer review [[Choudhury 2022 (Reionization Intro)]] was explicitly written in memory of his PhD supervisor T. Padmanabhan, reflecting both his intellectual debt to Padmanabhan and his positioning as a custodian of rigorous theoretical tradition in the field. This pedagogical choice—writing a comprehensive physics review—demonstrates his commitment to training the next generation of reionization researchers.

## Research Focus and Methodology

Choudhury's research spans several interconnected themes:

**Reionization Topology and Morphology:** He has investigated the spatial structure of reionization, including the inside-out versus outside-in topology question—that is, whether the universe reionizes starting from overdense regions (inside-out, driven by massive halos) or from underdense regions first (outside-in, driven by lower-mass sources). This distinction has profound implications for 21cm power spectrum predictions and the large-scale spatial fluctuations in the ionization field that the EFT framework aims to capture.

**Lyman-Alpha Forest Constraints:** His work on Lyman-alpha optical depth fluctuations at z ~ 5.5 has provided crucial observational constraints on reionization. The Lyman-alpha forest opacity becomes an increasingly useful diagnostic tool at higher redshifts where 21cm observations become challenging, and understanding the relationship between Lyman-alpha absorption and the underlying ionization field is essential for multi-probe constraints.

**Photon Budget and Halo Mass Functions:** The 2022 review provides detailed derivations of the photon budget (the balance between photon production from sources and photon consumption by ionizing gas) and the role of halo mass functions in determining which sources can reionize the universe. These calculations form the foundation for semi-numerical and fast simulation codes like 21cmFAST, which use excursion-set theory and halo populations to approximate the reionization dynamics. The photon budget framework is the physical starting point for understanding what an effective field theory of the ionization field should capture.

**Semi-Numerical Simulations:** Beyond full hydrodynamical simulations, Choudhury has contributed to semi-numerical methods—fast approximations that use N-body simulations to generate density fields and then apply analytic or semi-analytic rules for ionization. These methods bridge the gap between full physics simulations (like those using the ART code or CROC simulations) and extremely fast approximations (like 21cmFAST), making them valuable for understanding the physical underpinnings of faster codes.

**21cm Forecasts for Indian Facilities:** He has worked on forecasts for Indian radio telescopes, particularly the uGMRT (upgraded Giant Metrewave Radio Telescope), exploring the capacity of current and near-future Indian facilities to constrain reionization with 21cm tomography.

## Research Style

Choudhury is known for a pedagogically-oriented, first-principles approach to reionization physics. Rather than purely numerical simulation-driven research, he emphasizes understanding the underlying physics—deriving the fundamental equations, making explicit the assumptions and approximations, and clearly explaining the physical origin of observed phenomena. This is evident in his 2022 review, which provides extensive derivations and physical intuition alongside results. This approach makes his work particularly valuable for students seeking to build solid theoretical foundations rather than treating reionization simulations as black boxes.

## Key Contributions to the Field

**The 2022 Reionization Review:** While [[Choudhury 2022 (Reionization Intro)]] is already referenced in this wiki, it bears emphasis as his signature contribution. This is not merely a literature summary but a pedagogical derivation of key results: photon budgets, the role of stellar populations, the Lyman-alpha forest as a reionization diagnostic, theoretical frameworks for understanding reionization morphology, and connections to observables. The review has become a standard reference for anyone entering the field.

**Papers on Observational Constraints:** Beyond the review, Choudhury has authored numerous papers (referenced within his own review) on constraining reionization history from observations. These include detailed analyses of Lyman-alpha optical depth evolution, the interpretation of damping wings in quasar absorption spectra at z > 6, and the use of intergalactic medium absorption features to map out the ionization history.

**Connection Between Physics and Simulation:** His work consistently bridges analytical understanding and computational approaches, helping explain why specific assumptions are made in codes like 21cmFAST and what limitations these approximations impose.

## Relevance to This Thesis

Choudhury's work is foundational for an EFT-based thesis on reionization for several reasons:

1. **Photon Budget Framework:** The photon budget calculations presented in his review are the physical foundation upon which 21cmFAST builds. An EFT of the ionization field must ultimately respect the same conservation laws and physical constraints that Choudhury's photon budget captures. When the EFT introduces effective parameters (like the EFT stochastic term P_εε), these must be interpretable in terms of the underlying physics that Choudhury carefully derives.

2. **Understanding Halo Mass Functions:** The role of different halo masses in ionizing the universe, and how this determines the correlation structure of ionized regions, is essential input for understanding what scales the EFT needs to capture and what scales can be integrated out.

3. **Pedagogical Entry Point:** For a student learning the field, Choudhury's review is the single best pedagogical reference. It provides the clear derivations and physical explanations needed to understand why certain approximations are made in faster codes. This is invaluable when building an EFT, which is fundamentally about understanding which physics is essential and which can be coarse-grained.

4. **Topology and Morphology:** His work on reionization topology directly addresses the large-scale spatial structure of ionization fluctuations—exactly what the EFT expansion targets. Papers on inside-out versus outside-in reionization shed light on the expected topology of ionized bubbles, which affects the power spectrum and bispectrum of the ionization field.

5. **Multi-Probe Consistency:** His work connecting Lyman-alpha forest constraints to reionization physics emphasizes that a complete understanding requires consistency across multiple observational probes. An EFT framework must be testable against diverse observational constraints.

## Connections and Collaborations

Choudhury is embedded in the Indian and international reionization community. NCRA-TIFR itself is one of Asia's premier astrophysics institutes. He has collaborative connections to:
- The broader Indian radio astronomy community (uGMRT collaborations)
- International reionization theorists referenced in his review
- Students and postdocs who have trained in his research group

His work is widely cited by both simulation-focused groups (like Gnedin & Madau) and observational campaigns, positioning him as a bridge between theory and observation.

## Notes

Choudhury represents a traditional, first-principles approach to reionization theory, with roots in the classical theoretical tradition of Indian physics (via T. Padmanabhan). His emphasis on understanding photon budgets, halo populations, and reionization morphology provides the physical scaffolding that modern fast simulations like 21cmFAST rest upon. For a thesis on effective field theory of reionization, understanding the underlying physics that Choudhury carefully explains is essential: an EFT can only be a systematic approximation to exact physics if that exact physics is first clearly understood.
